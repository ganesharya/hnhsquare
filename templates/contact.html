FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Design Studio work?

Our AI Design Studio uses machine learning to analyze your room type, size, style preferences, and color choices. It then generates multiple design concepts with furniture arrangements, color palettes, and material recommendations tailored to your space.

Can I try the VR walkthrough before buying?

Absolutely! Our VR Walkthrough is completely free to try. You can explore pre-designed sample homes or customize rooms with your preferred colors and furniture. Book a demo through our contact page for a guided experience.

Do you offer installation services?

Yes, we offer end-to-end installation services across 50+ cities in India. Our trained technicians handle everything from delivery to final installation. Service charges vary based on location and project scope.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day return policy for unused products in original packaging. Custom-made items and installed products are non-returnable. Please contact our support team for specific cases.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope. A single room redesign typically takes 2-3 weeks, while a full home interior project may take 6-12 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during the consultation phase.

Do you provide material samples?

Yes! We provide free material samples for wood, fabric, and finish options. Simply request samples through our contact form or visit any of our partner showrooms to experience products in person.
