How to Export from Enscape

  1. Open your architectural model in Revit / SketchUp / Rhino / ArchiCAD
  2. Launch Enscape and set up your desired views
  3. Go to Enscape menu → "Web Standalone"
  4. Click "Export" and choose output folder
  5. Upload the exported folder to /static/enscape/your-project-name/
  6. Access via: /enscape-viewer?project=your-project-name
